In practice, each direct Dell selector or thermal table displays all 22 recommended model names in its corresponding system context. The 14 remaining candidate rows stay outside the recommendation.

## Corrections and publication limits

- **R6725 dimensions corrected.** The candidate formerly stated `42.8 x 482 x 816.921 mm with bezel`. Dell's [current R6725 product specification](https://www.dell.com/en-in/shop/ipovw/poweredge-r6725) states **42.80 × 482.00 × 817.89 mm with bezel**, and **815.10 mm without** (lines 201–213). The candidate now uses 817.89 mm, includes a direct HTML source, and marks the conflicting PDF search-index value as unsuitable for dimensions. Its 1U/two-socket metadata is independently supported by the system manual.
- **R470 option restriction added.** The two candidate rows and their `publishRecommendation` copies now say that Xeon 6 P-core requires the R1S option, together with the 350 W heatsink/fan requirement. The product page and thermal matrix are both attached as sources.
- **C6615 unit of account clarified.** The candidate now has `platformNote`: “One C6615 compute sled; the 2U C6600 enclosure holds up to four sleds.” Its `rackUnits` is intentionally omitted for the sled. Dell's thermal tables also restrict 96 GB Samsung DIMMs in some air-cooled configurations; the six CPU row notes retain that caveat.
- **PDF evidence boundary.** `dell-exp-r7715-guide` and `dell-exp-r470-guide` are official Dell PDFs surfaced through search indexing, but the direct PDF fetch returned 403 during this review. Their exact CPU rows are independently visible in Dell's direct HTML thermal matrices above. Treat the HTML matrices as the primary publishable evidence unless the PDFs are fetched and checked separately.
- **Configurator boundary.** R4715, R5715, and R6615 CPU options were checked on specific Dell US order pages. The candidate notes correctly preserve that selected configuration and regional availability may vary. The pages establish listed compatibility, not universal orderability.

## Hold audit and structural QA

The 14 rows outside `publishRecommendation` remain unapproved: five marked `needs_review`, four `search_index_only`, and five marked `source_row_verified` but held for the original Dell agent's unresolved platform/configuration criteria. This includes R6725–9575F: it appears in Dell's matrix, but some storage configurations require direct liquid cooling and some cells have a 30°C limit, so it was **not** promoted in this review. No held row was added to the recommendation.

I parsed the amended JSON, confirmed every platform source ID resolves, confirmed all 22 recommendation entries have matching candidate rows with identical source IDs and restriction notes, and recounted the 14 held rows. The candidate is a research artifact with `supportLevel: candidate` and no site `products` array; it is deliberately not a publish-ready platform snapshot. The site validator must run on the curated integration after research-only fields and held rows are removed.
