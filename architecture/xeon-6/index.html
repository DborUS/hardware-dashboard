START HERE · DECODE THE NAMES

One family, two naming axes

Read the name as product series → codename and package branch → core type. Granite Rapids and Sierra Forest are chip codenames. AP/SP identify package branches; P/E identify core types. The two labels answer different questions.

Xeon 6900P→Granite Rapids-AP→P-cores

P-core designs emphasize per-core and vector execution; E-core designs emphasize core density and parallel throughput. These CPUs do not mix the two core types.

BRANCHES IN THESE DIAGRAMS

Granite Rapids-SPXeon 6700P / 6500P · P-coresXCC: 2 compute dies; HCC/LCC: 1 each · 2 I/O dies · 8 DDR5 channels · HCC up to 48 P-cores / LCC up to 16
Sierra Forest-SPXeon 6700E · E-coresSeparate E-core product · up to 8 DDR5 channels

These views omit the distinct Xeon 6300P / Catlow and Xeon 6 SoC lines, 4- and 8-socket platforms, and Xeon 6+ / Clearwater Forest. “Sierra Forest-AP / 6900E” appears in older source material; Intel’s current Xeon 6 E-core catalog ↗ lists 6700E.
